# DazyFabrics
Consumer Insights and journey mapping for a E-Commerce Homeware website, complete with a dashboard (Sales and Marketing, UX) and a recommendation table / executive report write up.

**Business Need?**
>Your Senior MR Analyst asked you to act as a liaison for the sales and marketing team on deliverable prep for the upcoming VP presentation. The initial interactive and detailed deliverables will serve as a reference for the MR’s research objective. They also want a quick PowerPoint and document with insights and key points for sending off if asked.

> Note: This case study is based on a generated data set. Any business insights found should not be applied to any external market or industry.

**About The Data**
> This data compilation comprises two extensive consumer records tables within the Homeware industry. Each table captures comprehensive information, including Product Name, Price, Product Code, Customer Region, Consumer Channel, Campaigns, Customer Type, Total Sales, Order Timestamps, and other relevant data points.

**Business Questions?**
> How has the consumer journey evolved from 2024 to 2026, and how does this transformation contribute to the company’s projected 2026 sales growth?

> At what rate do promotional initiatives, such as affiliate programs, discount codes, and campaigns, facilitate this growth?

> For 2027, where should DazyFabrics allocate its digital marketing expenditure per region?
